# Mathematical Equations

SlideDown now supports beautiful math rendering with KaTeX!

## Inline Math

You can write inline equations like $E = mc^2$ or the quadratic formula $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$ directly in your text.

---

# Display Equations

## Pythagorean Theorem

$$
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
$$

## Quadratic Formula

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

---

# Calculus Examples

## Integral

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

## Derivative

The derivative of $f(x) = x^n$ is:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}(x^n) = nx^{n-1}
$$

---

# Advanced Examples

## Matrix

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
x \\
y
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
ax + by \\
cx + dy
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Summation

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$
